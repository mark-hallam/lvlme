How scores work: seven factors, each 0 to 5 from cited evidence, weighted into a fit score out of 100, then turned into chance bands. A separate sceptical reviewer must agree with every score above 2. The projection applies each action on its target date, weighted by its chance of working, with caps so it can't overpromise; a role with an eligibility blocker you can't clear never gains eligibility.

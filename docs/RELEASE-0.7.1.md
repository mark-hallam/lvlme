# LVLme 0.7.1: review fixes

5 November 2026. Fixes from three rounds of independent review of 0.7: a
Claude reviewer and a Codex reviewer scored nine journeys blind in a real
browser. How a score is worked out did not change.

- Goal reached is described as a measured level, not a job offer.
- One orange accent across Today and Mission control.
- When no date is expected for the next level, it says so plainly.
- On phones, a slimmer level-up banner and a cleaner header.
- The demo's Friday review matches its data, the goal-reached demo has a
  Level 3 goal, and the demo profile says its example.com links are made up.

Where it stands, honestly: after three rounds the reviewers score most
journeys 7 or 8 out of 10, short of the 9 we aim for. The open items are in
the [changelog](https://github.com/mark-hallam/lvlme/blob/v0.7.1/CHANGELOG.md)
history and are the next work. Tested on macOS; Windows is not tested yet.

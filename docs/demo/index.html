See LVLme with made-up data

For the job you can't get yet. LVLme works out honestly how far you are from a job, turns the gap into real actions, and measures again as you go. These are the pages it builds, for a fictional person.

Open a demo

  • LVLme quick: one job, honest odds

    One job link and a CV give honest odds and the top 3 fixes, in a couple of minutes. Alex Example asks about Deployment Program Manager, AI Agents at Globex Labs: fit 41 of 100 now, up to 56 if the two things it cannot check turn out strong. Interview chance low, offer chance very low.

  • The full campaign dashboard

    Five weekly runs at three organisations: Altitude, Flight level, Quests, Badges and what to do next. Latest run 1 November 2026: best match Program Manager, Robot Fleet Deployments at Initech Robotics, fit 57 of 100, Level 3, Moderate.

Everything here is made up

Alex Example, Acme AI, Globex Labs and Initech Robotics are fictional, and so are the job postings, the CV, the evidence and the scores. No real person's data is on these pages, and the odds say nothing about any real company's hiring.

Get LVLme

LVLme is free and open source. It runs in Claude Code, Codex or Cursor on your own computer, keeps your files there, and drafts everything: it never applies, sends or posts for you.

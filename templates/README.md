# This folder is private

This is your career-eval workspace. It holds your evidence, your contacts,
your scores and your plan.

- Keep it on your own computer. Do not put it in a shared folder, a public
  repository, or a git repository that has a remote (a copy on a server such
  as GitHub). The scripts refuse to run on a workspace in those places.
- Cloud AI agents send what they read to their provider. Point helper agents
  at `report/for-agents/` only, and keep sensitive mode on in `config.json`.
- Other agents may write only to `campaign/inbox.md`.
- Nothing here is ever applied, sent or posted for you. Drafts in
  `campaign/drafts/` are for you to read, edit and send yourself.

To delete your data, delete this folder.

Open your dashboard: `report/index.html`.

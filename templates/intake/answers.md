# Intake answers

Private. Your answers to the interview in `method/INTAKE.md` (in the engine
folder). Write in your own words. Leave a question blank if you would rather
not answer. Never include age, gender, ethnicity, disability, religion or
family status: the method does not use them.

Each heading has an anchor that evidence items can point to, for example
`intake/answers.md#work-rights`.
